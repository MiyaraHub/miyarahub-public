FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

How does FuryPath communicate with my HDFury device? +
FuryPath sends HTTP GET requests directly to your HDFury device on your local Wi-Fi network, using the same protocol used by HDFury's built-in web interface. Commands go to GET /cmd?key=value and status polling reads from GET /ssi/*.ssi endpoints. No external servers are involved. Your device must be on the same Wi-Fi network as your phone.
Which operating system versions are supported? +
FuryPath is available on iPhone, iPad, Android, Mac and Windows. iOS requires iOS 16.0 or later. macOS requires macOS 11.0 or later. Android requires Android 7.0 (API 24) or higher.
Can I control my HDFury device remotely from outside my home? +
FuryPath connects to whatever IP address you configure. If your HDFury device is accessible via VPN or Tailscale, the app will work remotely. We recommend using a VPN or Tailscale rather than exposing your device directly to the internet.
Does it support all HDFury models? +
FuryPath supports VRRooM, VERTEX2, VERTEX, DIVA, Maestro, ARCANA2, Arcana, and Dr.HDMI 8K. The app auto-detects your device on connection and activates the feature set relevant to your model. Not all models support all features. JVC Macros, for example, are available on VRRooM, VERTEX2, DIVA and Maestro only.
How much does FuryPath cost? +
FuryPath is a one-time purchase at $9.99. No subscription, no ads, no in-app purchases. You buy it once and own it.
Can I manage multiple HDFury devices? +
Yes. FuryPath supports multiple devices, each with its own settings, navigation tabs, and connection state. Switch between devices from the main dashboard with a single tap.
Can I customize the navigation and Home screen? +
Each device has a Home screen plus feature tabs (INFO, EDID, HDR/AVI, DV, CEC/eARC, MACROS, OSD, OLED, CONFIG), shown according to your model. You choose which tabs appear in the bottom navigation bar per device (Home plus up to 6 more, minimum 2), and you can reorder and hide the cards on Home. Each device remembers its own layout independently.
