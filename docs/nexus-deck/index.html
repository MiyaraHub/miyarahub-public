MiyaraHub / NexusDeck

NexusDeck · for Audyssey calibration filesEvery calibration, one deck.✦

A calibration file library for Denon and Marantz AV receivers with Audyssey MultEQ XT or XT32.

Keep .ady and .oca calibration files, Dirac Live projects and MultEQ-X .mqx files in one library. Compare curves, place gain cuts on .ady and .oca, send an .ady or .oca file to a speaker preset on your Denon or Marantz receiver, and send a Dirac Live capture you recorded yourself into a Dirac slot. NexusDeck does not measure your room or create curves.

Features

Keep, compare and send.

NexusDeck keeps the files the tools you already use make: Audyssey MultEQ Editor, A1 Evo and AcoustiX. It does not measure your room or create curves.

No. 01

◉ Every curve file in one library

Import .ady files from Audyssey MultEQ Editor and .oca files from A1 Evo and AcoustiX. Add folders, tags, ratings and notes, and search by file name, tool, receiver model, notes or tags. Share a curve file from its page.

▣ Measurements

Open .frd, REW text, Dirac Live .liveproject and MultEQ-X .mqx files, and view and compare their curves. REW .mdat is not read.

No. 03

◎ View and compare

See every channel on one chart, with impulse response and group delay. Export an image or CSV. With Pro, compare curves with a table of the difference.

No. 04

▢ Gain edits

Place gain cuts on a channel, from 0 to -12 dB, and see the result first. An edited .ady or .oca can be sent to Speaker Preset 1 only, as a computed filter, not measured. The stored file is never changed.

0 to -12 dBPreset 1File kept
No. 05

◆ Send to your receiver

NexusDeck finds your receiver on the network. Before anything is written, it checks the file against the receiver: the model, the MultEQ version and the channels. A mismatch is blocked with the reason. NexusDeck asks before it replaces a preset, then sends to the speaker preset you pick.

Speaker PresetChecked firstAsks first
No. 06

⚙ Dirac Live slots

Read the Dirac Live slots and switch between them. Replay a Dirac Live send you recorded yourself into a slot, and NexusDeck selects that slot and reads it back; the result says kept, not kept or unknown. That speaker preset then uses Dirac Live instead of Audyssey. NexusDeck builds, edits or converts no Dirac filter.

No. 07

◈ Presets and speakers

See which speaker preset is active and each speaker's distance and trim. Switch the preset, compare the curves last sent to two presets, and keep a backup: every .ady or .oca send saves the preset first. Levels, distances and crossovers are set in AVR Maestro for now (coming soon).

No. 08

▣ Preset backups

Before every .ady or .oca send NexusDeck keeps the target speaker preset's settings on your device, so you can see what a send replaced. Back up now keeps the active preset's settings without sending anything. Both are free.

How it works

From curve file to receiver in three steps.

Bring the curve file, look at it, send it. Try the whole flow first on the demo receiver inside the app.

1

Import your file

Import an .ady from Audyssey MultEQ Editor or an .oca from A1 Evo or AcoustiX. It joins your library with its channels, target curve and notes.

2

Look, compare, edit

Open a curve to see every channel on one chart. Compare it with another curve with Pro, or place a gain cut. The stored file is never changed.

3

Send to a speaker preset

Find your receiver, pick a speaker preset and send. Without Pro, sends go to Speaker Preset 1.

Security & Privacy

Local only. No account. No cloud.

Your calibration files stay on your device and your receiver stays on your local network. Nothing about your setup reaches MiyaraHub unless you send it to us.

◉

No account

No sign-up and no MiyaraHub login. MiyaraHub runs no server the app talks to.

▣

Files stay on your device

Your library, edits and settings are kept on your device. Nothing is synced to MiyaraHub.

◎

Your home network only

NexusDeck talks to your receiver directly on your local network. Calibration files never pass through a MiyaraHub server.

◆

Crash reports only if you allow them

On Android and iOS, crash reports go to Firebase Crashlytics only after you allow them on the first-run prompt. Turn them off in Settings > About. The Mac and Windows apps send none.

●

No analytics, no ads

No analytics library, no ads and no advertising ID.

◈

Logs only when you send them

Settings > About shares the app logs as one zip file for support, only when you choose to send it.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

Everything you need to know about NexusDeck.

Is NexusDeck affiliated with Audyssey, Dirac, Denon or Marantz? +
No. NexusDeck is an independent app by MiyaraHub Technologies LLC, not affiliated with or endorsed by Denon, Marantz, Audyssey or Dirac. All trademarks belong to their owners.
Which receivers work? +
For .ady and .oca, networked Denon and Marantz models with Audyssey MultEQ XT or XT32, with network standby on. Before a send, NexusDeck checks the file against the receiver and blocks a mismatch with the reason.
Which files does NexusDeck read? +
Curves, which can be sent: .ady from Audyssey MultEQ Editor and .oca from A1 Evo and AcoustiX. Measurements, to view and compare: .frd, REW text, Dirac Live .liveproject and MultEQ-X .mqx. Measurements and .mqx files hold no filters, so a file to send is an .ady or .oca. REW .mdat is not read.
Does NexusDeck replace MultEQ Editor or A1 Evo? +
No. NexusDeck does not measure your room or create curves. It keeps, compares and sends the files those tools make. Its gain edits only cut a filter a stored file already holds, and the result is kept beside that file.
Does it work with Dirac Live? +
Yes, to read and switch the Dirac Live slots and to replay a Dirac Live send you recorded yourself, not to make filters. NexusDeck replays the send into a slot, selects that slot and reads it back; the result says kept, not kept or unknown. That speaker preset then uses Dirac Live instead of Audyssey.
Does NexusDeck check a file before it sends it? +
Yes. Before anything is written, it checks the file against the receiver: the model, the MultEQ version and the channels. A mismatch is blocked with the reason, and nothing is written.
How much does NexusDeck cost? +
NexusDeck is a free download. Without Pro it pairs one receiver plus the demo, keeps three imported curve files plus the sample and sends to Speaker Preset 1, with 15 sends in all, Dirac Live sends included. Pro is one in-app purchase, $19.99 in the US, and adds unlimited sends to any preset, unlimited receivers and curve files, multi-curve comparison, the speaker preset switch in the top bar, Quick / Smart Select, Batch Map and library backup files. Preset backups, taken before each .ady or .oca send or with Back up now, are free.
Is there a user guide? +
Yes. The NexusDeck user guide is at nexusdeck.app.
