Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 15, 2026. Version 3.2.

This policy says what personal data MiyaraHub Technologies LLC handles, why, who receives it, how long it is kept and what you can ask us to do. It covers every app listed in Appendix A, the Elan Audio Lab store, this website and email you send us. Appendix A gives the details for each app. If this page and an app's in-app text differ, write to us and we will correct the one that is wrong.

1. Summary

  • Most of our apps control devices on your own network. The addresses, names and passwords of those devices stay on your phone or computer unless you send them to us yourself, for example in a diagnostic email, or unless your phone's own backup copies them.
  • Most apps send crash reports to Google Firebase Crashlytics. In most apps this is on by default. Some apps have no switch to turn it off. The table below and Appendix A say which.
  • Some apps send usage analytics to Google Firebase Analytics. Some ask first; some do not. The table below says which.
  • Crash reports and analytics are linked to random identifiers created on your device, not to your name. They are still personal data under laws such as the GDPR, and we treat them that way.
  • We do not sell personal data. We do not show ads in our apps.
  • The Elan Audio Lab store handles customer accounts, orders and payments. It is described in its own block in Appendix A and in section 14.

4. The data we handle, by source

4.1 Email you send us

When you write to us, we receive your email address, your name if you give it, your message and any attachments. We use them to answer you.

4.2 Diagnostic reports you choose to send

Many apps have a button that prepares a diagnostic email or file. It opens your own email app or share sheet, and nothing is sent until you press send. Depending on the app, the report can contain your device model and system version, app settings, logs, and the network addresses, hardware (MAC) addresses, host names, model names and serial numbers of the devices you control. Some apps remove some of these first; many do not. Appendix A lists what each app includes. Read the report before you send it, and remove anything you do not want to share.

4.3 Crash reports (Firebase Crashlytics)

When an app crashes or hits a serious error, Crashlytics sends Google a report with the error and the code location, the app version, the device model, the system version, free memory and storage, a random Crashlytics installation identifier and a Firebase installation identifier. Google receives your IP address when the report is uploaded. In many apps, the error text can contain the network address or name of a device you control. Appendix A says, for each app, whether crash reports are on by default, where the switch is, and what is removed before sending.

4.4 Usage analytics (Firebase Analytics)

In the apps where it is active, Firebase Analytics sends Google events such as first open, session start, screen views, in-app purchases and the app events Appendix A lists. Each event carries a random app instance identifier, a Firebase installation identifier, the device model, system version, language, app version and an approximate location (country or region) that Google derives from your IP address. On iOS it can also use the vendor identifier. In apps where Appendix A says so, it also collects the Android Advertising ID. We use analytics to see which features are used and where apps fail. We do not use it to show ads.

4.5 Purchase and store reports

When you buy an app or an in-app upgrade, Apple, Google or Microsoft processes the payment. They send us sales reports with dates, countries, products, prices and order numbers. We do not receive your card details. Apps keep a record of your purchase on your device so they can unlock features; Appendix A says where.

4.6 The Elan Audio Lab store

The store handles accounts, orders, payments, shipping addresses, reviews, newsletter sign-ups, contact forms, quote requests, warranty registrations, giveaways and artist applications. See section 14 and the Elan Audio Lab block in Appendix A.

4.7 This website

GitHub Pages hosts miyarahub.com. GitHub receives your IP address and browser details when you load a page and keeps logs for security. Our fonts are served from miyarahub.com itself, so no font request goes to a third party. Our pages set no cookies and run no analytics. If you pick a language, your choice is saved in your browser's local storage under the key "lang" and never sent to us.

4.8 Data that stays on your device

Device addresses, settings, passwords and tokens you enter are stored on your device. We do not receive them unless you send them to us in a diagnostic report. Your device's own backup (iCloud, Google, or another backup tool) may copy them; Appendix A says which apps exclude what from backup.

9. Your rights

Wherever you live, you can ask us to tell you what personal data we hold about you, to correct it, to delete it, or to stop using it. Email admin@miyarahub.com. We answer within 30 days. 11. Security and breaches

Data sent to Firebase, the Elan Audio Lab store and its payment providers travels over encrypted connections. Several apps store passwords in the system's secure storage. Some do not; Appendix A says which. Many devices you control, such as AV receivers, projectors and UPS units, use network protocols without encryption, so other devices on your network may be able to read that traffic. No method is completely secure.

If a breach of personal data affects you, we will tell you and the authorities as the law requires, including within 72 hours to the supervisory authority under the GDPR when it applies, and within 60 days to affected Texas residents, with notice to the Texas Attorney General within 30 days when 250 or more Texans are affected.

Almanox

Name and platforms
Almanox, a sky and astronomy planner for Android and iOS. Not yet released.
Data model
Your locations, photos and settings are stored on your device. The app fetches weather, maps, terrain, satellite and aurora data from public services on the internet. There is no Almanox account and no MiyaraHub server.
Network destinations
MET Norway (api.met.no) receives the latitude and longitude of the active location rounded to about 11 meters, automatically and about every 3 hours. Open-Meteo receives the text of a place search. The Apple or Google geocoder receives exact coordinates or address text when a place is named. OpenStreetMap (map tiles), Amazon Web Services (terrain tiles about 39 km wide), CelesTrak (ISS data, nothing sent) and NOAA (aurora forecast, nothing sent) are also contacted, and Firebase only after you opt in.
Crash reports
Default: off. Switch: the consent prompt and Settings, one switch for both crash reports and analytics; turning it off deletes unsent reports. What is sent: the error message and stack, plus a Firebase installation ID, device model, OS version and app version, and Google sees your IP address. Redaction: not confirmed; the audit found no custom keys or user ID added to reports.
Analytics
Off until you opt in. The same switch in the consent prompt and in Settings controls it. Identifiers: app instance ID and Firebase installation ID; whether the Android Advertising ID is collected has not been confirmed. Events: app_open, screen_view and the automatic Firebase events.
Permissions
Android: internet, camera (sky finder), precise and approximate location (sky calculations), notifications (sky event alerts) and start at boot (to reschedule alerts and widgets). iOS: camera, photo library, location when in use and calendars. There is no background location.
Purchases
One optional one-time purchase, Almanox Pro, through the App Store or Google Play. The unlock is cached on your device.
Local storage and uninstall
Saved locations, the active location, settings, place photos, a map tile cache and a forecast cache are stored on your device. Android backup is turned off. On iOS this data is included in iCloud and device backups. Calendar events you add and files you save survive uninstall, and there is no single delete-all action.
Egress to services you choose
Opening a dark-sky site in Google Maps sends its coordinates to Google. A backup file you export contains all saved locations and goes wherever you share it.
Support email contents
The "Send all relevant files" action emails a ZIP to admin@miyarahub.com through your mail app. It always includes the active location name with latitude and longitude to about 1 meter, and a list of app preferences that contains all saved locations. There is no option to leave a part out.
Known limits
Location leaves the device to MET Norway, the platform geocoder and the map and terrain tile hosts. The support ZIP always carries your locations. iOS backups include saved locations and photos.
