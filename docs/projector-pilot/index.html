How it works

Direct network connection. No cloud required.

Projector Pilot PJLink & VP21 talks directly to your projector on your local network. There is no MiyaraHub server and no account.

1

Find your projector

Scan your network for projectors answering on the PJLink (4352) and ESC/VP.net (3629) ports, or enter the IP address. The app detects the model and the features it supports.

2

Connect on your network

The app uses ESC/VP21 over ESC/VP.net (TCP 3629) and PJLink (TCP 4352) for power, inputs and settings, and Epson Web Control (HTTP 80, Digest sign-in) where the projector offers it. Everything stays on your local network.

3

Take full control

Adjust picture settings, switch inputs, control the motorized lens, manage keystone correction, monitor lamp hours, and navigate the OSD - all from one elegant app.

Diagnostics

Firebase Crashlytics sends crash reports, linked to a random ID, not your name, so we can fix bugs. Usage analytics are off until you opt in. No account and no ads.

◆

Passwords in secure storage

Projector passwords are kept in your device's secure storage; addresses and app preferences stay in local app storage. Nothing is synced to a MiyaraHub server.

▢

Home Automation only when you set it up

With the Home Automation purchase, the app sends to the web addresses you enter, the Hubitat hub you link and the Home Assistant you sign in to. Without it, nothing leaves for a smart home service.

☼

Network permission only

The app uses network access only to reach your projector on your local network, and notifications only for maintenance reminders you set.

Frequently asked questions.

Is there an Epson projector app for iPhone and Android? +
Yes. Projector Pilot PJLink & VP21 runs on iPhone and iPad (iOS 15 or later), Android, Mac (macOS 12 or later) and Windows, from the App Store, Google Play, the Mac App Store and the Microsoft Store. It controls Epson projectors on your local network.
Does it work with the Home Cinema 5050UB, the LS12000 or the QB1000? +
Yes. The EH-QB1000 is confirmed on a real projector, and the app carries profiles for the EH-LS12000 / Pro Cinema LS12000 and the EH-TW8400 / Home Cinema 5050UB, along with the other models listed under Compatibility. The app reads the model when it connects and shows only the controls that model supports.
Is it an alternative to Epson iProjection? +
They do different jobs. Epson iProjection sends your screen or photos to the projector. Projector Pilot PJLink & VP21 does not project content; it is a remote and settings controller for power, inputs, picture, HDR, lens, keystone and maintenance, with scenes and Home Automation.
Which projectors are supported? +
Networked Epson home cinema projectors, from the EH-QB1000 and the LS12000, LS11000, LS800 and LS500 lasers to the 6050UB, 5050UB, 4010 and 3800 lamp models and earlier 6040UB, 5040UB and 5030UB generations. Smaller Home Cinema, LS300, LS650 and EF models get power and input. The Home Cinema 3200 has no network control. Baseline PJLink control also works with projectors from other makers.
How does it communicate with my projector? +
Three ways, all on your local network: ESC/VP21 over ESC/VP.net on TCP port 3629 (with the projector's password if you set one), PJLink on TCP port 4352 for power, inputs and status, and the Epson Web Control interface on HTTP port 80 with Digest sign-in for the settings it exposes.
Can I control the motorized lens? +
Yes, on projectors with a motorized lens. You get lens shift, focus and zoom, and you can save and recall lens memory presets for different aspect ratios or screen positions. Lens controls are hidden on models without a motorized lens.
Does it support HDR and wide color gamut? +
Yes, on models that offer them. You can choose the dynamic range (Auto, SDR, HDR PQ and HLG, where the model lists them) and the color space, and adjust per-channel RGB gain and offset for calibration.
Can I power on the projector from standby? +
Yes, as long as the projector keeps its network active in standby. Wired LAN and the projector's standby network setting make wake from standby reliable.
How much does Projector Pilot PJLink & VP21 cost? +
It is free to download, with one projector and basic control included. Pro is a $9.99 one-time unlock, Home Automation is $2.99, or get both as a bundle for $11.99. No subscription, no ads. You buy it once and own it.
